Źródła testów statystycznych używanych w analizie

1. Test normalności Shapiro-Wilka

Shapiro, S. S., & Wilk, M. B. (1965). An analysis of variance test for
normality (complete samples). Biometrika, 52(3/4), 591–611.
DOI: 10.1093/biomet/52.3-4.591

2. Test Manna-Whitneya U (Wilcoxon rank-sum)

Mann, H. B., & Whitney, D. R. (1947). On a test of whether one of two random
variables is stochastically larger than the other. The Annals of Mathematical
Statistics, 18(1), 50–60.
DOI: 10.1214/aoms/1177730491

Oryginalny test rangowy (alternatywne cytowanie):

Wilcoxon, F. (1945). Individual comparisons by ranking methods. Biometrics
Bulletin, 1(6), 80–83.
DOI: 10.2307/3001968

3. Korekta Bonferroniego

Dunn, O. J. (1961). Multiple comparisons among means. Journal of the American
Statistical Association, 56(293), 52–64.
DOI: 10.1080/01621459.1961.10482090

Oryginalna praca:

Bonferroni, C. E. (1936). Teoria statistica delle classi e calcolo delle
probabilità. Pubblicazioni del R Istituto Superiore di Scienze Economiche e
Commerciali di Firenze, 8, 3–62.

4. Implementacja (SciPy)

Virtanen, P. et al. (2020). SciPy 1.0: fundamental algorithms for scientific
computing in Python. Nature Methods, 17, 261–272.
DOI: 10.1038/s41592-019-0686-2
